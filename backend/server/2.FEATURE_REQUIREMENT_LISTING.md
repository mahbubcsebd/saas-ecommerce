# Mahbub Shop - Feature Requirement & Listing

This document outlines all the user-facing functional requirements and modules that need to be implemented (or are already implemented) on the Frontend (E-commerce Storefront, Admin Panel, and POS interface) based on the backend architecture.

---

## 1. E-Commerce Storefront (Customer Facing)

### Authentication & User Profile
- **Registration & Login:** Standard email/password login with JWT support.
- **Password Recovery:** OTP-based verification for forgotten passwords.
- **User Dashboard:** 
  - View Order History and tracking status.
  - Submit and track Product Return Requests (RMA).
  - Manage saved shipping/billing addresses.
  - View and manage Wishlist items.

### Product Browsing & Interaction
- **Homepage:** Dynamic banners, featured categories, and trending products.
- **Product Listing Page (PLP):** Advanced filtering by categories, price range, stock availability, and custom attributes (Color, Size, etc.).
- **Product Detail Page (PDP):**
  - Dynamic variant selection (changes price/stock dynamically).
  - Image gallery with zoom capabilities.
  - Customer reviews and ratings.
  - "Out of Stock" alerts and "Add to Wishlist" buttons.

### Cart & Checkout
- **Cart Management:** Add, remove, or update quantities. Warn if stock is insufficient.
- **Coupon System:** Option to apply promotional codes or see automatic BOGOF (Buy One Get One) discounts.
- **Checkout Flow:**
  - Support for both logged-in users and **Guest Checkout**.
  - Dynamic shipping cost calculation based on Zone.
  - Payment gateway integration (SSLCommerz, bKash, Nagad, Stripe, COD).

---

## 2. Admin Dashboard (Management Interface)

### Analytics & Reporting
- **Dashboard Overview:** Real-time summary cards for Sales, Orders, and Active Customers.
- **Financial Reports:** Visual charts for Profit/Loss, Revenue, and Vat.
- **Expense Reports:** Detailed breakdown of operational costs filtered by date and category.

### Product & Inventory Management
- **Catalog Management:** Create and edit Categories, Brands, and Products.
- **Variant Setup:** Add multiple attributes (Size, Color) and link specific images and prices to variants.
- **Stock Movement:** Manually add stock (Stock-in).
- **Wastage & Damage Reports:** Record damaged items, specify the reason, and auto-deduct from stock with loss calculation.

### Order & Due Management
- **Order Processing:** View incoming orders and update lifecycle statuses (`PENDING` → `PROCESSING` → `SHIPPED` → `COMPLETED`).
- **Due Collection:** View `PARTIAL` or `UNPAID` orders and log new payments (Installments) until the `dueAmount` becomes 0.
- **Invoicing:** Generate, view, and print professional PDF invoices.
- **Return Management:** Approve or reject customer RMA requests and adjust inventory.

### Expense Management
- **Expense Categories:** Setup categories like "Office Rent", "Electricity", "Staff Food".
- **Daily Expenses:** Input daily expenses with amounts, dates, and reference vouchers.

### Marketing & Communications
- **Discount Engine:** Create percentage/flat coupons or complex BOGOF rules.
- **Campaigns:** Send bulk Emails and SMS to specific customer groups.
- **Page Builder:** Use GrapesJS to drag-and-drop custom landing pages.

### System & Staff Administration
- **Staff Roles:** Create Admin, Manager, and Staff accounts with specific permissions.
- **Activity Logs:** Track which staff member updated an order or modified a product.
- **Settings:** Configure store name, logo, shipping zones, tax rates, and email/SMS API keys.

---

## 3. Point of Sale (POS) Interface

### Fast Sales Operations
- **Barcode & Search:** Rapidly add products to the cart via barcode scanner or fast search.
- **Walk-in Customers:** Process orders without creating a full user account (take only Name & Phone).
- **Cart Management:** Hold carts, clear carts, or apply instant POS discounts.

### Checkout & Payment
- **Payment Split & Due:** Handle partial cash payments. System auto-calculates `tenderedAmount` and `changeAmount`. If payment is less than total, the rest is marked as `dueAmount`.
- **Receipt Printing:** Auto-generate and print thermal POS receipts upon order completion.

---

## 4. AI & Advanced Modules

- **AI Customer Chatbot:** An integrated floating widget on the storefront to answer customer queries.
- **AI Admin Assistant:** A search bar in the admin panel to type queries like "Show me sales from last month" and get automatic charts.
- **Auto-Translation:** One-click AI translation for product descriptions and UI text.
